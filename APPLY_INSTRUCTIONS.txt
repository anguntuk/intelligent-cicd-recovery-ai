Behavior
  1. No rule matches -> category=unknown.
  2. Analyzer writes unknown_triage metadata and keeps AI disabled.
  3. The workflow sets approval_required=false when any analysis is unknown.
  4. A dedicated unknown-failure-triage job creates a report/checklist artifact.
  5. First approval, second approval, watchdog, remediation, commit, push and
     post-push CI dispatch jobs are explicitly excluded for unknown failures.
  6. The incident ends at manual engineering investigation; no approval,
     source edit, patch, commit, push or retry is requested or performed.
